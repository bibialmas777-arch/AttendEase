Faculty Guidelines

Attendance records are governed by the academic council minimum threshold. When conducting lectures:

  • Mark attendance within 24 hours of lecture completion.
  • Edits to past sessions require formal justification captured in the audit log.
  • Verify duty leave and medical certificates before approving grievance dispute requests.
