Minimum Attendance Threshold

Updating this regulation immediately recalculates compliance thresholds across all student dashboards, triggers warning alerts, and updates semester exam clearance criteria. Every modification is immutably logged to the audit ledger.

%
Resulting Status Classification Engine:
GREEN ≥ 85.0% (Threshold + 10%) — Safe & Commended
YELLOW 75.0% - 84.9% — At-Risk / Watchlist
RED < 75.0% — Defaulter / Exam Barred

Regulatory Directives

  • The University Grants Commission (UGC) mandates a standard 75% classroom presence for semester end examinations.
  • When the threshold is adjusted, all student Recovery Planners dynamically recalculate needed consecutive sessions.
  • Duty leaves and medical absences approved via the Grievance Portal are credited as official attendance presence.
Immutable Audit Logging All threshold modifications record old value, new value, administrator identity, and precise timestamp in the MySQL audit ledger.
