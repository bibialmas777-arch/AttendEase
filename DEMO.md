# AttendEase — Presentation & Live Demonstration Script

This structured script provides a complete walk-through to demonstrate AttendEase during a presentation or viva examination.

---

## 1. Introduction (1 Minute)
1. **Title**: AttendEase — Smart Student Attendance Management System.
2. **Context**: Second-year CSE Mini-Project built with **Java 17**, **Spring Boot 3**, **Spring Security**, and **MySQL 8**.
3. **Core Philosophy**: Zero business logic in JavaScript. All threshold policies, consecutive recovery math, authorization ownership checks, OpenPDF report generation, and audit logging execute on the secure Java backend.
4. **Design System**: Burgundy Glassmorphism (`#641B32`, `#3B1022`, `#1C0B14`, frosted glass, WCAG AA contrast).

---

## 2. Student Experience Demonstration (3 Minutes)
1. Open browser at `http://localhost:8080/login.html`.
2. Click the quick-fill button **Student 1** (`student1@attendease.com` / `Student@123`).
3. Note the role selector tab is set to **Student**. Click **Sign In**.
4. **Student Dashboard**:
   - Point out the **Overall Attendance Gauge** showing a circular SVG progress meter with the Java-evaluated status color (RED, YELLOW, or GREEN).
   - Point out the **Warning Banner**: "Overall attendance is below the requirement. Attend the next 16 classes consecutively to recover."
   - Review the subject performance table with individual progress bars and status badges.
5. **Recovery Planner (Key Feature)**:
   - Click **Recovery Planner** in the sidebar (or click the **Plan** button beside Data Structures CS201).
   - Enter/select **Target = 75%**.
   - Show the test case output ($C=40$, $A=26$, $t=75$):
     - Consecutive Classes Needed: **16**
     - Expected Percentage After Consecutive: **75.0%**
     - Safely Skippable Classes: **0**
     - Guidance message generated by Java: *"You need to attend the next 16 classes in a row to reach 75%."*
   - Change target to **80%**:
     - Consecutively needed increases to **30**.
   - Select another subject where attendance is high (e.g., 90%):
     - Consecutive needed = **0**, Safe misses = **8**.
6. **Attendance History**:
   - Navigate to **Attendance History**.
   - Filter by date range or specific subject.
   - Click **Dispute** on an absent lecture to demonstrate the grievance link.
7. **Grievance Portal**:
   - Open **Grievance Portal**.
   - View previously submitted grievances and their faculty review statuses (Approved / Under Review / Pending).
8. **Reports**:
   - Click **Attendance Reports** and click **Download PDF Statement**.
   - Open the generated PDF: observe the formal header, student roll number, subject table, and printable light layout.
   - Click **Download CSV Spreadsheet** and verify the file opens in Excel or text viewer.

## 3. Faculty Experience Demonstration (3 Minutes)
1. Sign out of Student portal.
2. Click **Faculty 1** (`faculty1@attendease.com` / `Faculty@123`) and select **Faculty** tab. Click **Sign In**.
3. **Faculty Dashboard**:
   - View assigned courses (CS201 Data Structures, Section A & Section B).
   - View pending grievances counter.
4. **Mark Attendance (with Duplicate Prevention)**:
   - Click **Mark Attendance**.
   - Select Course: *Data Structures CS201 — CSE-2 [Section A]*.
   - Select Date: *Select today's date*.
   - Click **Load Student Roster**.
   - Toggle individual students between **Present** and **Absent** or click **Mark All Present**.
   - Click **Submit & Save to Database**.
   - *Duplicate Prevention Demonstration*: Attempt to mark attendance again for the same course, section, and date.
   - Note the immediate rejection: *"Attendance for Data Structures & Algorithms in Section A on [Date] has already been submitted. Use the Edit Attendance feature to modify records."*
5. **Edit Past Attendance & Audit Logging**:
   - Go to **Class History**.
   - Click **Edit** on a past student record.
   - Change status from ABSENT to PRESENT.
   - Enter reason: *"Medical slip verified retroactively."*
   - Save: Note the success message confirming an immutable audit trail entry was recorded.
6. **Review Grievances**:
   - Navigate to **Review Grievances**.
   - Locate the pending grievance submitted by Student 1.
   - Click **Review**.
   - Change decision to **APPROVED**, enter remarks: *"Duty leave certified by HOD."*
   - Click **Commit Decision**.
   - Explain: The Java backend updates the attendance status in the database in a single transaction, inserts an audit log, and dispatches a notification to the student.

## 4. Administrator Experience Demonstration (3 Minutes)
1. Sign out and click **Admin** (`admin@attendease.com` / `Admin@123`). Select **Admin** tab and sign in.
2. **Administrative Control Center**:
   - Total students (40+), total faculty, total classes, and college-wide attendance percentage.
   - Real-time **Defaulters Summary** list.
   - **Recent Audit Trails** stream showing past attendance edits, grievance approvals, and threshold updates with timestamps and user names.
3. **Dynamic Threshold Configuration**:
   - Navigate to **Threshold Settings**.
   - Change the minimum threshold from **75%** to **80%**.
   - Click **Update Policy & Commit Audit**.
   - Return to **Dashboard**: Notice that students who were previously yellow (e.g. 78%) now immediately shift to RED defaulters!
4. **Student & Faculty Management**:
   - Go to **Students**: demonstrate search filter and click **Add Student**.
   - Demonstrate the safe soft-deactivation (sets `active = false`), protecting historical attendance from FK violations.
5. **Course Allocations**:
   - Go to **Assignments**: demonstrate allocating an instructor to a course and section.
6. **Analytics & Intelligence**:
   - Navigate to **Analytics**.
   - Review the **Monthly Trajectory** smooth gradient line chart, **Present vs Absent** ratio doughnut, and **Course Average** bar chart.
7. **Defaulter Reports Export**:
   - Navigate to **Reports & Export**.
   - Download the official **Defaulters PDF Report** and **Defaulters CSV**.
